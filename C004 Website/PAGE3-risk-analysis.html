Risk Analysis & Decision Dashboard

Calculated system tiers and prioritized action protocols based on household vulnerabilities.

High Risk Critical Priority Rescue (Life-Threatening)

Immediate dispatch of priority tactical rescue teams and watercraft. The household is designated as trapped with extreme mobility blockages or contains an unaccompanied vulnerable minor.

📋 What to do:

  • Due to Inability to Evacuate (Q6): Clear immediate physical pathways to structural heights or roofs to await watercraft deployment.
  • Due to Stranded Minor Status (Q7): Secure vital personal identification and school documents in a strapped, body-worn pouch.
🚨 Emergency Notice: Structural entrapment identified. Call national responders at 911 immediately.
High Risk Medical Priority Rescue (Medical Emergency)

Immediate dispatch of emergency medical personnel or specialized rescue squads equipped with basic life support assets to safely relocate individuals with advanced health requirements who cannot move independently.

📋 What to do:

  • Due to Elderly/Disabled Members (Q3): Group all specialized mobility assets (walkers, canes, wheelchairs) right against the primary exit threshold.
  • Due to Medical Assistance Needs (Q8): Keep patients comfortable, completely dry, and isolated from ambient flood exposure.
🚨 Emergency Notice: For sudden medical status deterioration or unresponsiveness, dial 911 instantly.
High Risk Standard Priority Rescue (High Risk Housing)

The household is queued systematically into the primary evacuation roster due to structural and environmental vulnerabilities (coincidence of low-lying terrain and river proximity) compounded by a lack of personal transit assets.

📋 What to do:

  • Due to Low-Lying/River Risks (Q1 & Q4): Shut down the main residential electrical breakers and disconnect secondary plumbing input valves before water penetrates the perimeter walls.
  • Due to No Vehicle (Q9 Unchecked): Consolidate the family Go-Bag containing clean bottled water, non-perishable food, and backup power banks.
🚨 Emergency Notice: Monitor local levels. If structural boundary lines breach completely, contact 911.
Low Risk Trigger Early Evacuation Warning

Automated SMS alerts or community sirens are instantly dispatched to the target household because geographical risk parameters (low-lying or proximity to a river) or household vulnerabilities match active threat profiles, requiring proactive preparation before risks escalate.

📋 What to do:

  • Due to Official Warning Status (Q5): Check community radio feeds or local municipal announcement boards continuously for route updates.
  • Due to Geographical Vulnerabilities (Q1 & Q4): Elevate sensitive ground-level appliances, digital gear, and high-value appliances onto upper furniture lines.
Low Risk Log as Self-Evacuating / In Transit

The system registers that the household possesses private vehicular transport and has confirmed receipt of the warning. Status is updated to 'In Transit' to prevent redundant deployment of field rescue units.

📋 What to do:

  • Due to Private Vehicle Availability (Q9): Top up vehicle fuel cells completely and verify that an updated automotive tool set, tire tools, and flashlights are positioned inside the trunk locker.
  • Map out alternative dry navigation tracks to stay clear of known low-clearance overflow areas.
Low Risk Monitor and Prepare (Stable Risk Profile)

Environmental conditions and vulnerability attributes are currently non-critical. No active field rescue units are required at this time.

📋 What to do:

  • Due to Baseline Stable Standing: Keep localized municipal disaster hotlines handy and secure outdoor materials on patios to avoid blocking storm drains.
  • Inspect household emergency lanterns, replace worn-out batteries, and check first-aid boxes.
