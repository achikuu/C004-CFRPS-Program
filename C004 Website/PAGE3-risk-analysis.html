Risk Analysis & Decision Dashboard

Calculated system tiers and prioritized action protocols based on household vulnerabilities.

ℹ️ System Preview Note: Below are the 5 potential prioritized decision profiles. When connected to data metrics, the system dynamically displays the exact matching condition tier.
Critical Threat

CRITICAL RESCUE PRIORITY (LIFE-THREATENING)

Condition Checklist: Priority rescue is requested, the household cannot evacuate on its own, or an unaccompanied minor is stranded.

Action Protocol: Immediate deployment of tactical water rescue craft and high-clearance emergency assets to coordinates.
Medical Urgent

MEDICAL PRIORITY RESCUE

Condition Checklist: An immediate medical emergency exists, or vulnerable occupants (elderly/disabled) are present without self-evacuation capability.

Action Protocol: Alert and dispatch a specialized medical response team accompanied by standard rescue services.
Elevated Risk

STANDARD PRIORITY RESCUE (HIGH-RISK ZONE)

Condition Checklist: Household is within a low-lying zone near an active river system and lacks personal vehicle transport assets.

Action Protocol: Log into the primary BDRRMC evacuation routing queue for systematic municipal team deployment.
Early Warning

TRIGGER PROACTIVE EVACUATION WARNING

Condition Checklist: An official warning has been broadcast, and the household resides in a low-lying area or river proximity but is not yet trapped.

Action Protocol: Push automated SMS advisory alerts and activate local neighborhood sirens for early preparation.
Monitored

LOG AS SELF-EVACUATING / IN TRANSIT

Condition Checklist: Household possesses a functional transport vehicle and has confirmed preparation status.

Action Protocol: Register household route trajectory as 'Self-Evacuated' to clear administrative field resources for more vulnerable cases.
