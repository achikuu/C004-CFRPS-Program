CFRPS: CAINTA FLOOD RESCUE PRIORITIZATION SYSTEM

Many families living near rivers and in low-lying areas are highly vulnerable to flooding. Delayed evacuation and the lack of proper identification of high-risk households can result in injuries, property damage, and loss of life. This project aims to develop a logical system that can identify households requiring priority assistance before and during flooding events. The system will help barangays improve evacuation planning, disaster preparedness, and emergency response efforts, reducing the risk of accidents and casualties during typhoons. Through this system, authorities can determine which households need immediate rescue and send early warnings to residents located in flood-prone and low-lying areas.

“The local government of Cainta, Rizal, on Wednesday declared a state of calamity due to flooding brought by heavy rains from the southwest monsoon (Habagat) and tropical cyclones Crising and Dante on July 23, 2025. The Sangguniang Bayan noted that the weather disturbance caused the displacement of numerous families, loss of income, and disruption of essential services in the municipality’s seven barangays.”

“Hundreds of families are still staying in government shelters in Cainta, Rizal on Tuesday after heavy rains brought by Tropical Storm Enteng caused flooding across the entire municipality on September 3, 2024.”
