About CFRPS

Cainta Flood Rescue Prioritization System structural overview matrix.

System Purpose & Description

CFRPS acts as a digital decision platform mapping terrain vulnerability logic against dynamic family data vectors. By applying continuous condition verification steps, it eliminates human sorting triage processing errors under high-stress weather event response cycles.

Cainta Flood Vulnerability Realities

Geographically nestled inside a natural lowland basin valley bounded by the Marikina watershed and Laguna de Bay basins, the Municipality of Cainta routinely experiences rapid stormwater accumulation cycles. Historical event tracking maps show sectors matching low elevation points suffer flash inundations within hours of peak rainfall volume generation.
